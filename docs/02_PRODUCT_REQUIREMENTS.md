# 02 — Product Requirements Document

## Product name

Working title: **Bluegrass Gameday**

## One-sentence product

A Kentucky-first fan app combining sports stats, matchup dashboards, predictions, rewards, recruiting/local pipeline coverage, and Pregame-style watch-party discovery.

## Target users

### Primary
- Kentucky Wildcats football and basketball fans
- Fans who follow KSR/On3, ESPN, X/Twitter, message boards, and recruiting updates
- Fans who like comparing stats and talking matchups
- Casual fans who want stats explained in simple terms

### Secondary
- Kentucky high school sports fans
- Parents/families of local athletes
- Former players and alumni
- Local venues/bars/restaurants that host watch parties
- NIL sponsors and local businesses

## Jobs to be done

1. "I want to know what matters today for Kentucky sports."
2. "I want to see the next game and understand the matchup."
3. "I want stats that are powerful but not overwhelming."
4. "I want to make predictions and compete with other fans."
5. "I want to follow recruits, transfers, and Kentucky high school talent."
6. "I want to find where other fans are watching the game."
7. "I want fun, collectible achievements around my fandom."

## MVP features

### Home / Pulse
- Daily Kentucky sports pulse card
- Next game countdown
- Recent scores
- Top stat of the day
- Fan poll
- Prediction prompt
- News link cards, admin-curated or allowed RSS
- XP progress

### Teams
- Kentucky football
- Kentucky men's basketball
- Basic support for adding women's basketball, baseball, volleyball later
- Schedule, roster, standings, team stats, leaders

### Gameday HQ
- Game card
- Opponent matchup
- Team comparison
- Key players
- Win probability where licensed/available
- Fan prediction pool
- Keys to the game
- Venue/watch-party placeholder

### Stats Lab
- Football:
  - record,
  - scoring offense/defense,
  - yards per play,
  - EPA/PPA if available,
  - success rate,
  - red zone,
  - turnovers,
  - explosive plays,
  - opponent-adjusted metrics.
- Basketball:
  - record,
  - adjusted offensive/defensive rating,
  - tempo,
  - effective FG%,
  - turnover rate,
  - offensive rebound rate,
  - free throw rate,
  - shot profile,
  - lineup/player cards if available.
- Explain every advanced metric in plain language.

### Predictions
- Free-to-play only
- Questions:
  - winner,
  - score margin,
  - first team to score,
  - leading scorer,
  - total three-pointers,
  - rushing yards range,
  - turnover count,
  - fan confidence.
- XP and badges
- Weekly and season leaderboards

### Community
- Polls
- Reactions
- Comments on game threads
- Moderated fan posts
- No unmoderated direct messaging in MVP

### Recruiting / NIL / Transfer Portal
- Commitments list
- Transfer tracker
- Public prospect pages
- NIL news link cards
- Player profile pages
- No copying paywalled content
- Link to sources

### High School / Bluegrass Pipeline
- School pages
- Schedules/scores link-outs
- Stats leaders, if import/permission available
- Local player spotlights
- UK-related pipeline watchlist
- No direct messaging minors
- Strong moderation

### Profile
- Favorite sports
- Favorite high school/team
- Prediction record
- XP level
- Badges/cards
- Notification settings

### Admin portal
- Manage games
- Manage teams/players
- Feature stat cards
- Manage polls/predictions
- Moderate content
- Manage high school profiles
- Manage news link cards
- Trigger data sync jobs
- View analytics

## Non-goals for MVP

- Real-money sports betting
- Unlicensed live video streaming
- Scraping paywalled sites
- Official UK branding without license
- Open direct messaging
- Full nationwide high school database
- Automated recruitment ranking of minors without oversight
- Complex fantasy leagues

## Success metrics

### MVP
- 500 TestFlight/beta users
- 30% weekly active users
- 50+ prediction submissions per Kentucky game
- Average 3+ sessions per game week
- 10+ local schools/high school pages manually curated
- 5+ sponsor/venue leads

### Phase 2
- 5,000 users
- 1,000+ prediction submissions per major game
- 100+ high school teams followed
- 25+ local venue/watch-party pages
- First paid subscribers or sponsor revenue

## Product principles

1. Make stats feel fun.
2. Keep Kentucky at the center.
3. Be faster than a website and deeper than a score app.
4. Use data ethically.
5. Avoid licensed-brand problems early.
6. Design for mobile first.
7. Build for eventual Pregame integration.
