# 10 — Monetization and Growth

## Monetization philosophy

Launch free and valuable. Add premium once fans have a daily habit.

## Revenue streams

### 1. Premium subscription

Recommended:
- $2.99/month
- $19.99/year
- optional $29.99/year Founding Fan plan with early badge

Premium features:
- deeper Stats Lab
- custom stat alerts
- ad-free
- advanced matchup reports
- historical comparisons
- more prediction analytics
- profile cosmetics
- premium badges/card packs, not player likeness unless licensed
- AI-generated matchup summaries from licensed/public data

Use RevenueCat for mobile subscription management.

### 2. Local sponsorships

Best fit for Chris's Pregame experience.

Sponsor products:
- "Presented by" stat cards
- watch-party sponsorship
- Friday night high school scoreboard sponsor
- rivalry week sponsor
- fan challenge sponsor
- coupon/reward cards

### 3. Venue/watch-party plan

Reuse Pregame concepts:
- venue profile
- map/listing
- Kentucky watch party
- fan check-ins
- sponsored placement
- coupon/reward
- analytics dashboard

Possible pricing:
- free basic listing
- $49/month local featured venue
- $149/month city featured partner
- $499/season Kentucky watch-party package

### 4. Affiliate revenue

Possible:
- ticket affiliate links
- merchandise affiliate links, only licensed vendors
- food delivery/promos
- travel/hotel around games

### 5. NIL/local business

Only with permission:
- athlete-approved promotions
- local sponsor pages
- NIL education content
- official links

## Growth strategy

### Phase 1: community seeded by Chris
- Post game predictions on TikTok/Reddit/X
- Share stat cards
- Use KSR-style conversational voice without copying content
- Promote during football/basketball game weeks
- Run "Can you beat the app?" prediction challenge

### Phase 2: high school/local
- Invite fans to follow their local Kentucky high schools
- Friday night scoreboard
- Sweet 16 content
- local sponsor cards

### Phase 3: venue network
- Connect to Pregame venues
- show places in Lexington/Louisville/NKY/Spartanburg if desired
- offer watch-party packs

## Viral loops

- Share prediction card
- Share badge card
- Share stats "Kentucky edge" card
- Invite friends to leaderboard
- Venue check-in leaderboard
- "Represent your high school" profile badge

## Launch content calendar

### Football season
- Monday: recap + leaderboard
- Tuesday: stat trend
- Wednesday: matchup edge
- Thursday: prediction challenge
- Friday: high school pipeline
- Saturday: gameday live
- Sunday: postgame badges

### Basketball season
- Day before: matchup card
- Game day morning: keys to game
- During game: fan pulse
- Postgame: Four Factors recap
- Weekly: transfer/recruiting radar

## Avoid early mistakes

- Do not lock basic scores/schedules behind paywall.
- Do not overbuild high school features before data rights are clear.
- Do not market as official Kentucky app.
- Do not spend heavily on ads until prediction loop is proven.
- Do not let community content go unmoderated.
