## Important legal / data rules

- Do not scrape paywalled or copyrighted sites such as KSR+, On3, 247Sports, Rivals, ESPN, or MaxPreps.
- Use official APIs, licensed feeds, RSS feeds where allowed, user-entered data, or admin CSV imports.
- Treat University of Kentucky marks/logos/colors/trademarks as protected. Do not use official UK logos, official Wildcats artwork, or the exact official athletics brand package without permission.
- Present the app as an independent fan project unless an official license or partnership is obtained.
